## 1 · The decode step is not linear at batch 1

| batch | measured (ms) | pre-registered linear fit (ms) | measured by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 63.6 | 54.1 | W1, median of 360 requests |
| 1 | 64.1 | 54.1 | decode sweep |
| 2 | 47.8 | 55.4 | decode sweep |
| 4 | 58.0 | 57.8 | decode sweep |
| 8 | 57.2 | 62.8 | decode sweep |
| 12 | 67.6 | 67.7 | decode sweep |
| 16 | 75.7 | 72.6 | decode sweep |

One sequence decodes at **63.6 ms** per token (W1, never below 60.5) — *slower* than two to eight sequences. A line through the sweep cannot express that; hence R² 0.58. The cause is not established here (CUDA graphs were captured for batch 1). What is measured: a lone prefill pass has at most ~33 ms of fixed cost (§4, HTTP round trip included), and it reads every weight once, as a decode step does — so the extra ~30 ms per batch-1 decode step is not memory bandwidth.

## 3 · W1's histogram residual is the timing fit, not the cache model

| run | model timing | cache hits | max CDF distance | ≤ 2 s cumulative, engine/model |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1_long_r1 | pre-registered fit | 28,304 | 0.042 | ≤0.2s 29/0 · ≤0.5s 79/0 · ≤1s 96/79 · ≤2s 119/117 |
| W1_long_r1 | batch-1 step = measured (post-hoc) | 28,304 | 0.015 | ≤0.1s 0/10 · ≤0.2s 29/26 · ≤0.5s 79/50 · ≤1s 96/67 · ≤2s 119/119 |
| W1_long_r2 | pre-registered fit | 28,304 | 0.042 | ≤0.2s 33/0 · ≤0.5s 79/0 · ≤1s 93/79 · ≤2s 119/118 |
| W1_long_r2 | batch-1 step = measured (post-hoc) | 28,304 | 0.014 | ≤0.1s 0/8 · ≤0.2s 33/25 · ≤0.5s 79/52 · ≤1s 93/68 · ≤2s 119/119 |
| W1_long_r3 | pre-registered fit | 28,304 | 0.042 | ≤0.2s 30/0 · ≤0.5s 79/0 · ≤1s 89/79 · ≤2s 119/118 |
| W1_long_r3 | batch-1 step = measured (post-hoc) | 28,304 | 0.018 | ≤0.1s 0/11 · ≤0.2s 30/25 · ≤0.5s 79/46 · ≤1s 89/65 · ≤2s 119/119 |

The residual sits entirely in the ~119 shortest-lived events: each request's last decode blocks, recycled by the next request. The fitted batch-1 step is too short, so in the simulation every request finishes early and idles until the next real send — ageing exactly those blocks. Replacing that one number with its measured value moves them back; **the cache hits do not change under either timing**, as the pre-registration claimed.

## 4 · Why prefill_base_s came out negative

TTFT for a lone request = **33.3 ms** + 0.701 ms × uncached token (R² 0.932). The simulator emits the first token at the end of a step costing prefill(uncached) + decode(1), so the procedure subtracts the fitted decode(1) = 54.1 ms — more than the whole intercept. In the engine the first token comes out of the prefill pass itself; there is no separate decode step to pay. So the simulator's step model **double-charges the fixed per-pass cost on every step that prefills**. Harmless where queueing dominates (W3's TTFTs are 15–41 s); a known over-estimate of ~20 ms on an idle replica. Recorded as a limit; not re-tuned.
