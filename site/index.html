kv-routing-sim

Which KV-cache routing policy should you run — and on how many GPUs? A simulator whose cache model was checked against a live vLLM, and whose tests were written down before they ran.

Keep each user on one GPU — until your tenants' system prompts outgrow one GPU's KV cache. Then route by prefix, and keep its load weight small.

Scope, before you trust it. Generated agentic traffic, Qwen3-4B, T4 timings. The direction of this flip survived a 2× and 4× faster GPU and a 2× cheaper or dearer prefill; the replica counts did not. On real traces — Mooncake, whose prompts are shared across users, and AgentX's coding-agent sessions, where nothing is — session-sticky and prefix-affinity came out within one replica of each other: the many-prompt advantage is not yet shown on real traffic. Prefix-affinity without a load weight failed only where prompts were shared. On two live T4s the hit rates matched the simulator within 1.4% and the policies kept their order, but latency was 5–222× worse than predicted: the T4 counts below are simulator figures, and optimistic.
